# Islamic online-learning visual refinement

The page uses custom scenes created with the built-in imagegen tool. These are fictional representative visuals, not records of TGA sessions and not portraits of actual TGA students or teachers. Per the user's request, the page has no image labels or attribution captions. Copy does not identify the depicted people as actual participants. This document records provenance for maintainers.

The full-width dark hero preserves the existing donation state, handlers, amounts, IDs, availability checks and monthly management. Its integrated scene, gradient and low-opacity geometric linework use the existing navy, sage, parchment and restrained warm accent palette. Mission, funding, programme and closing scenes share a consistent online Islamic learning direction.

Assets live in `public/images/mission/`: `islamic-learning-banner.webp`, `islamic-remote-teacher.webp`, `islamic-learning-access.webp`, `islamic-digital-resources.webp`, `islamic-girl-online-learning-niqab.webp`. The programme story includes a girl in hijab and a female educator in niqab, alongside the boy and male educator scenes elsewhere. Original PNGs remain in the imagegen output directory. WebP optimisation resizes assets without semantic edits. Existing unused stock images remain available for rollback.
