## 1. Scope and priority

This policy applies only to the Sīrah | Beyond Chronology programme and the specific version accepted with the student's registration. The programme runs for a maximum of twelve months. Registration alone does not confirm payment or activate paid access.

For this programme, the rules below replace conflicting general TGA policy statements that monthly instalments are compulsory and that no refund is possible after fourteen days. They do not retrospectively change another programme's agreement. A student's mandatory consumer rights always take priority.

## 2. Monthly payments and cancellation

Monthly payments are voluntary renewals. Students may stop future renewals without becoming liable for the remaining twelve-month programme fees. Twelve billing cycles are the maximum programme schedule, not a twelve-payment debt.

For an automatic subscription, request cancellation through the saved registration's billing controls. TGA confirms cancellation after the provider verifies that future billing has stopped. Contact payment support promptly if cancellation cannot be confirmed. Stopping a subscription does not itself refund a completed monthly period.

Following cancellation, course and recording access continue until the end of the student's verified paid period, then end. Manual monthly payments do not create automatic debits. Statutory refund rights and remedies for service failure are preserved.

## 3. Annual upfront payments and unused months

Annual upfront is one payment for twelve months, without recurring monthly billing. A student may request an early cancellation and a refund review for the fully unused months of their paid annual period.

Under the standard rule, a partially started month counts as used. The calculation uses the actual verified upfront amount paid, not the undiscounted monthly price or Pakistan's higher manual monthly rate:

**Eligible refund = upfront amount paid × eligible fully unused months ÷ 12, less refunds already issued.**

The calculation rounds down once to the smallest billing-currency unit and cannot exceed the remaining amount collected. Staff check payment, dates, previous refunds and applicable legal exceptions. For example, GBP 323.89 with six eligible unused months gives GBP 161.94 before subtracting previous refunds. If statutory rights require a different result, they prevail.

After an approved annual cancellation, access to teaching and recordings ends at the current paid-month boundary recorded in the cancellation confirmation. Remaining eligible months are refunded. Sending a request alone does not approve a refund, change access or stop a separate provider arrangement.

## 4. Fourteen-day rights and the general policy

The general policy's blanket refusal of refunds after fourteen days does **not** cut off this programme's unused-month arrangement. That arrangement remains available after the initial fourteen days, subject to its eligibility rules.

Any applicable statutory cooling-off right is additional to these arrangements. For UK distance contracts for services, the normal cancellation period is measured from contract conclusion, not automatically from the first lesson. Early service performance, proportionate charges and any separate digital-content consent require the legally appropriate disclosures and express consent. Simply registering, accessing recordings or ticking the general terms must not be treated as an automatic waiver of statutory rights.

In addition, TGA provides **a full refund when cancellation is requested within fourteen days of Sīrah course commencement**. This additional contractual protection applies to Sīrah and is not limited to fully unused months. It does not shorten the statutory cancellation period measured from contract conclusion, or displace any other mandatory remedy. Staff must assess both protections and apply the result that preserves the student's rights; a standard unused-month calculation must not reduce an eligible full refund. The course commencement date must be communicated with the booking and checked from the actual cohort record, not inferred from the payment date.

For applicable UK service contracts, a request to begin during the statutory cancellation period must be express and separate from general terms acceptance. Where proportionate service charges would otherwise be lawful, they must not reduce TGA's additional promised full refund during its course-start protection window. For recordings supplied as digital content, any legally required prior express consent to immediate supply and acknowledgement of loss of the relevant statutory digital-content cancellation right must be separate, clear and confirmed on a durable medium. Such acknowledgement must not be presented as waiving the additional TGA full-refund promise or other statutory remedies. Missing consent must not be inferred from registration or playback. Consumer-law review must confirm classification of the combined live-service/recordings supply and final disclosures before publication.

## 5. Failed monthly renewal and recovery

The grace period is seven calendar days from the verified renewal due date. While the grace period applies, paid access continues. If payment remains unpaid after the deadline, access pauses. Repeated failed attempts do not restart the grace period.

Where supported, update or resume the existing subscription using the saved registration. An activation response or browser return alone does not establish payment. Access resumes only after successful payment is verified. A cancelled, expired or completed subscription is not restarted as a duplicate subscription.

## 6. Recordings and access dates

Recordings are available only while the student's paid entitlement is active, including any approved renewal grace period. No additional recording-access extension is promised beyond the recorded entitlement or original programme expiry. The registration and confirmation must show the applicable access dates. The current implementation begins the paid-period schedule on the first verified payment; this is separate from the actual cohort commencement date used for the additional fourteen-day full-refund protection. The booking confirmation must state both dates clearly.
